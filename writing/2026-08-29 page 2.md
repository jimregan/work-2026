One guiding principle has been to consider failure the default case — rather than trying to account for failure in retrospect, every step is assumed to have failed unless it declares otherwise. Partly, this is a result of experience — files being deleted during a move between network storage devices, site unavailability at scrape time, etc. — but in larger part because the intended use case of corpora that are extended incrementally differs from a failure only in terms of intent: to the underlying system, it makes no difference if the outputs are missing because a tool failed, or if they did not exist.
