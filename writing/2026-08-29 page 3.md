
## Sustainability

Sustainability is one of the driving motivators of this work: to be able to transition from a snapshot-oriented approach, with ad hoc processing, to an incremental approach with  transformations that are repeatable by design.

The transforms are intended to be driven primarily through configuration files, so that the variables and implementation are held separate, without restricting understanding to programmers. The metadata that drives the graph is not merely held apart for convenience, but so the differences between similar configurations can be compared easily: by isolating the key-value pairs from the code, the difference can be trivially computed and displayed.
