Although the best-known crawling efforts are broad, such as Common Crawl, for linguistic purposes more focused crawls are typically employed, scraping particular sites known to be high-value sources.

In the area of speech, such focused crawls are often found, with particular sites used as a source of data for speech recognition and speech synthesis. One of the best-known examples of this is LibriSpeech, which crawled LibriVox for public-domain audiobooks, and Project Gutenberg for the texts the readers used as source material.

Building on this, LibriTTS(-R) (the Google one) repeated this crawl, enhancing the audio and replacing the chunks of LibriSpeech with whole sentences.
