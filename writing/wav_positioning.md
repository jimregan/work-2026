Research and Positioning Strategy for Web-Oriented Standoff ASR Corpus Construction at WaC-13Strategic Positioning and Workshop AlignmentThe 13th Web-as-Corpus Workshop (WaC-13), co-located with EMNLP 2026 in Budapest, Hungary, provides a premier venue for computational linguistics research addressing the complete lifecycle of web-derived data. Endorsed by the ACL Special Interest Group on Web as Corpus (SIGWAC), the Common Crawl Foundation, and the OpenEuroLLM initiative, the workshop highlights a major paradigm shift toward Data-Centric AI. This framework moves away from uncurated, web-scale data scraping under the historical "more is better" assumption, prioritizing instead high-quality filtering, structural enrichment, legal compliance, and ethical data management.Speech recognition (ASR) corpus construction has traditionally remained distinct from web text corpus engineering. While text corpus research under SIGWAC has established methodologies for boilerplate removal, domain filtering, and copyright-compliant dataset distribution, ASR research continues to depend heavily on centralized, directly hosted audio-transcript repositories. This reliance introduces legal liabilities and bandwidth challenges when scaling up to web-derived multimedia content. Submitting a paper on web-oriented standoff annotations for ASR corpus creation directly addresses WaC-13’s core mandate by extending web corpus methodologies into multimodal speech data processing.This research strategy builds on the structural precedent of the Georgetown University Multilayer (GUM) corpus, specifically the UD_English-GUMReddit treebank published at WAC-XII. In UD_English-GUMReddit, proprietary text tokens are replaced with empty fields or placeholders in the public repository, while an automated fetching script (get_text.py) populates the underlying text on the user's local machine via third-party APIs or proxies. Extending this dynamic re-hydration model to multimodal speech data by decoupling structural and temporal annotations from raw audio-visual content provides a reproducible blueprint for web-derived speech corpora.Submission Tracks and TimelinesAligning the research lifecycle with the official publication schedule of WaC-13 at EMNLP 2026 is critical for peer review and publication. Standoff annotation decouples raw source data from markup layers, storing annotations as pointers into primary media assets.Multi-Layer Standoff Alignment ModelTo maintain synchronization across web HTML layouts, extracted character sequences, and acoustic media streams, the architecture extends the W3C Web Annotation Data Model. Each utterance in the corpus is represented by three interconnected annotation targets:Structural DOM Selector Layer: Uses CSS Selectors (e.g., article > div.transcript > p:nth-child(4)) to isolate transcript elements within dynamic web pages, removing non-linguistic web page elements like navigation bars and headers.Textual Character Offset Layer: Uses a TextPositionSelector (defining absolute start and end character indices within the targeted DOM node) combined with a TextQuoteSelector (containing prefix, exact, and suffix character sequences). The legal argument centers on the distinction between distributing copyrighted works and distributing structural and spatial metadata.Under United States Copyright Law (17 U.S.C. § 107) and the European Union Directive on Copyright in the Digital Single Market (Directive 2019/790), original text transcripts and recorded audio streams constitute protected expression 1 . However, spatial offsets, CSS selector paths, and temporal audio timestamps represent non-copyrightable factual facts and coordinates 2 .By publishing only the standoff metadata layer, the dataset provider avoids making unauthorized copies or distributions of protected expression. The end-user executes the fetching script locally, downloading the media file and HTML transcript directly from the source server using their own IP address and client session. The local system then performs character extraction and audio segment slicing according to the standoff coordinates.In the European Union, this local processing step is protected under Article 3 (Text and Data Mining for Research Institutions) and Article 4 (General TDM Exception) of Directive 2019/790. In the United States, local alignment and processing for non-commercial scientific research fall under Fair Use principles, as the end-user creates a transformative local asset without substituting for the commercial market of the original host page.Regarding platform Terms of Service (ToS) and the Robot Exclusion Protocol, centralized scraping systems often run afoul of redistribution restrictions. Client-side re-hydration scripts can integrate rate-limiting, custom User-Agent identification, and automated robots.txt compliance checking. This design adheres to the ethical data collection standards advocated by SIGWAC and the Common Crawl Foundation.Section-by-Section Paper Blueprint and Research NarrativeThe proposed 8-page long archival paper should follow a structured narrative that connects computational linguistics, legal compliance, and speech engineering.Section 1: Introduction and Related WorkThe introduction outlines the challenges of acquiring audio-visual data for ASR, contrasting the availability of online speech with the legal constraints on distributing raw multimedia files.
